# Third-party components

The native reader uses jsoup 1.18.3 (MIT), https://jsoup.org/ . Its original licence is bundled in app assets as jsoup-LICENSE.txt. AndroidX/Jetpack Compose and Kotlin dependencies retain their respective notices.

News: Economic Times, Mint, India Today and NDTV.com / NDTV Profit public RSS feeds, attributed with direct source URLs. The app is a personal reader; public availability is not a licence for commercial republication. Publishers can restrict access or return incomplete content. Article text is fetched on demand, is not bundled or sent to a Bigbull server, and no paywall bypass is attempted. Images are loaded from validated HTTPS publisher URLs in memory.

Price and statements: Yahoo Finance public endpoints; NSE daily files; MMI: published Tickertape source. Access remains best effort and subject to source terms. No exchange real-time licence is claimed.

The old Mozilla Readability/WebView extraction path was removed in version 0.7.
